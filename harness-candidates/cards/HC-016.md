## 目的

    Render SVG files faithfully to PNG at several sizes and build a labelled contact sheet

## 使った手段

    python3 -I render.py <svg-dir> --sizes 512,64,16 --sheet sheet.png  # librsvg + cairo via ctypes, montage with ImageMagick

## 既存ツールで足りなかった理由

    No SVG renderer CLI was available and the ImageMagick internal renderer is limited, so a script calling librsvg and cairo through ctypes was needed.

## 提案する引数と戻り値

    render_svg(paths, sizes, sheet_path)
