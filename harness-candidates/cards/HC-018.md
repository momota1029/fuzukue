## 目的

    Render SVG icon variants to several PNG sizes and build a labeled comparison sheet

## 使った手段

    convert -background none -density 384 IN.svg big.png; convert big.png -filter Box -resize NxN OUT-N.png; convert OUT-64.png -sample 192x192 mag.png; convert CELLS +append row.png; convert ROWS -append sheet.png

## 既存ツールで足りなかった理由

    No tool renders local SVG files at multiple sizes or composes a side-by-side sheet with magnified small sizes.
